Our Story

HOW IT ALL BEGAN

We first crossed paths in 2014, when I (Jane) transferred from BulSU Main to the BulSU Meneses Campus. Back then, we lived in different worlds — different circles, different interests, and even different crushes. Falling for each other was the last thing on our minds. But life has its own way of surprising us.

Through unexpected turns, we found comfort in each other’s company. What started as simple conversations turned into late-night talks, secrets shared, and laughter only we understood. Before we knew it, we became each other’s safe place.

By 2015, friendship blossomed into love. Our journey, like any other, had its share of sweet moments and silly fights — all witnessed by our closest friends. And now, after ten years of laughter, lessons, and countless memories, we’re ready to take the next step — to build our forever together. 💍❤️
